Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To advance the academic, technical, professional, and collaborative growth of all Health Informatics students through innovation, research, and practical application of health information technology.

  • Promote academic excellence in health informatics
  • Facilitate research and innovation opportunities
  • Build meaningful industry connections
  • Drive social impact through healthcare technology

Our Vision

To create a vibrant ecosystem where health informatics students develop cutting-edge solutions that transform healthcare delivery and improve patient outcomes through technology and data-driven decision making.

  • Innovation-driven problem solving mindset
  • Global perspective with local relevance
  • High professional and ethical standards
  • Inclusive, collaborative community culture
